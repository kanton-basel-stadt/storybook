# 2.4. How the Unplugin and Storybook play together

The Unplugin serves all the assets (CSS, fonts, icons, videos, etc.), whereas Storybook showcases different components
with HTML.

The Unplugin is meant to provide a unified CI/CD to several clients, Storybook itself is also a client and uses the
Unplugin for both its own appearance and the component styling.

Storybook itself does have little to no own styling (except to overwrite parts of its own theme) and, therefore, showcases
itself how a client could use the Unplugin with additional CSS.
