# 2.1.2. How to develop a new showcase page

Showcase pages are pages within Storybook. They consist of multiple different components and aim at showcasing
how these components work in their respective contexts.

Usually a showcase page consists of the header, content and footer. The content can differ widely and consist of either
simple or very complex structures.

Showcase pages are components and stories themselves. They are located under `stories/6_ShowcasePages`.

So, to learn how to build components and stories, please refer to [2.1.1 How to develop a new component](/docs/2-tutorials-1-contribution-tutorials-1-how-to-develop-a-new-component--docs).
